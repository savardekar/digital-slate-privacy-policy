📱 Privacy Policy – Digital Slate

📅 Last Updated: 22 July 2026  |  SarvmTech Digital Slate

SarvmTech ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Digital Slate mobile application.

📌 Important: Digital Slate is a drawing & writing app (digital blackboard). It does not require login, registration, or any personal information to function.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Digital Slate collects minimal data to improve user experience:

  • ❌ No Personal Data – We do not collect name, email, phone, address, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • 📱 Device Information – Model, OS version, screen resolution (for analytics and compatibility).
  • 📊 Usage Analytics – App opens, feature usage, crash logs via Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics.
  • 📂 Storage Access – Only when you choose to save or share your drawings (with your explicit permission).
  • 🛒 Advertising ID – Used by AdMob to show relevant ads (non-personalized by default).

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

  • To provide core functionality – drawing, coloring, erasing, saving, and sharing.
  • To analyze app performance, fix crashes, and improve features.
  • To display advertisements (if applicable) and measure ad performance.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

3. Permissions We Request

Digital Slate asks for the following permissions (only at runtime):

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – To load existing drawings from your device (if any).
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – To save your drawings to your device's gallery / storage.
  • INTERNET – For ads and analytics (no personal data is transmitted).

All permissions are requested with a clear explanation, and you can deny them without breaking core drawing functionality (except saving).

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party SDKs to enhance our app:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics) – Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob – Google Privacy Policy

These services may collect device information and usage data as per their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

  • All your drawings are stored locally on your device – we have no servers, so we do not store any user-generated content.
  • You can delete your drawings at any time via your device's file manager or by uninstalling the app.
  • Since we don't store personal data, there is nothing to "delete" from our side.

6. Children's Privacy

Digital Slate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
