Meta Niche Analyzer

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Meta Niche Analyzer is a Chrome extension designed to help users research advertising competition across different niches using publicly visible information from the Meta Ad Library.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

2. Information We Access

The extension accesses publicly visible advertising information from Meta Ad Library pages, which may include:

  • Advertiser and business names.
  • Advertisement IDs and links.
  • Advertisement text and creative-related information visible on the page.
  • Advertisement status and start dates.
  • Landing page URLs and domains.
  • Impression information when made available by Meta.
  • Niche and category research information.

This information is used solely to provide niche research, advertiser tracking, competition analysis, and related features within the extension.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Meta Niche Analyzer does not intentionally collect:

  • Personal emails, phone numbers, or home addresses.
  • Passwords or authentication credentials.
  • Private Facebook messages or personal communications.
  • Payment card or banking information.
  • Health or sensitive personal information.
  • General browsing history outside the extension's research activity.

4. Local Data Storage

Research information and extension preferences are stored locally in your browser using Chrome storage.

This may include:

  • Selected niches and categories.
  • Advertiser and advertisement research records.
  • Historical scan information.
  • Scan schedules and extension settings.
  • Dashboard and competition analysis data.

This locally stored information helps maintain your research history between browser sessions.

5. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the extension is used to:

  • Identify unique advertisers within selected niches.
  • Analyze advertising competition.
  • Track advertising activity over time.
  • Calculate niche research and opportunity metrics.
  • Display research insights in the extension dashboard.

6. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell user data or share locally stored research information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We do not use collected information for creditworthiness assessment, lending decisions, or unrelated purposes.

7. Third-Party Websites

The extension interacts with Meta Ad Library to access publicly visible advertising information.

Meta operates independently and has its own privacy policy and terms of service. Your interaction with Facebook and Meta services is governed by their respective policies.

Meta Niche Analyzer is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.

8. Data Security

Research data is stored within your browser using Chrome's storage facilities. Users should maintain appropriate security for their browser and device.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored research data remains in your browser until it is deleted through available extension controls or browser storage management.

Uninstalling the extension may also remove its locally stored extension data.

10. Children's Privacy

Meta Niche Analyzer is intended for business owners, marketers, advertisers, and market researchers. It is not designed specifically for children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
